Facts: Challenger Deep ~10,935 m · pressure rises ~1 atm per 10 m (≈1,070 atm at the bottom ≈ 1 t per cm², about one fingernail) · at 10 m the air in your lungs is squeezed to half (Boyle's law) · sunlight is mostly gone below ~200 m, none below ~1,000 m · deepest scuba dive: 332 m (Ahmed Gabr, 2014) · sperm whales dive to ~2,000 m and hunt squid · Titanic wreck ~3,800 m · deepest fish filmed: snailfish at 8,336 m (2022) · a plastic bag was recorded at 10,898 m (Chiba et al., 2018, Marine Policy). Surviving such a dive without a submarine is impossible: this is a "what if".
